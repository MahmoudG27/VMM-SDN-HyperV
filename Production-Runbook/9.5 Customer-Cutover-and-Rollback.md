## 2. Cutover sequence

| # | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freeze changes. Final backup of what can't be rebuilt | Customer / us |
| 2 | Power off the source VMs. Convert the remaining VMs (9.4) | Us |
| 3 | **NSX Tier-0 stops advertising the public /32s** | The provider network team |
| 4 | Confirm on the router: no route to the /32s | The provider network team |
| 5 | Create the NAT connection and VIPs (9.2 §4). **Our MUX advertises the /32s** | Us |
| 6 | Confirm on the router: each /32 via both MUX | The provider network team |
| 7 | Pattern B: firewall DNAT rules active. Run Apply `-CheckOnly` (drift + policy checks) | Us / customer |
| 8 | Test every public port from outside (L12, L27), outbound from a VM, VPN if any | Us |
| 9 | Application tests | Customer |
| 10 | Go / no-go at the rollback deadline | PM |

## 3. Rollback

The VMware VMs stay **powered off** until the rollback period ends. Don't delete them.

| # | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remove the public-IP objects (MUX stops advertising) | Us |
| 2 | Confirm the /32s are withdrawn | The provider network team |
| 3 | NSX Tier-0 advertises the /32s again | The provider network team |
| 4 | Power off the Hyper-V VMs, power on the VMware VMs. Reinstall VMware Tools if needed | Us |
| 5 | Test, inform the customer, record the reason in As-Built | Us |

Data written on Hyper-V after cutover is lost on rollback unless copied back. The customer accepts this at go/no-go.

## 4. Post-cutover

- [ ] Backups of the customer VMs configured (product and retention per the provider).
- [ ] Monitoring added.
- [ ] Pattern B: **UDR guard running** for this customer.
- [ ] Pattern B: **VIP backend check** clean (Apply `-CheckOnly` exit 0).
- [ ] 9.3 §3 network check clean. Cost Center and Tag set.
- [ ] End of rollback period: delete the VMware VMs, release NSX objects (customer + the provider approval).
